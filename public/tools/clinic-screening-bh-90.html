How to use it

Each box drops down the boxes underneath it. Opening one branch closes its siblings, so what stays on screen is the single path you are actually on.

One reminder at a time — if two screens are positive, finish this path, then reopen from the top for the second. Show every branch opens the whole flowsheet at once as a reference map.

Verify before this goes live

Transcribed from the handwritten BH-90 flowsheet. Four items were hard to read — confirm them against the original before staff rely on this:

  • PACT referral list — read as "PCP, OM/RN, SW". Confirm what OM stands for on your unit.
  • ED Mental Health backup — read as pager 5117 / ext. 2613. Confirm the pairing.
  • MHTC — read as Mental Health Treatment Coordinator, and as required on every decline.
  • Suicide risk levels — low / moderate / high all route to the same offer of discussion on the sheet. Confirm that high risk is not meant to skip straight to ED Mental Health.
