FAQ
Do I need to connect my POS system?

No. MenuPulse works independently. Upload invoices manually (photo or PDF) and we handle the rest. No IT setup, no integrations to break.

How do you track competitor menus?

We monitor their online presence — website menus, delivery platform listings (DoorDash, UberEats, Grubhub), and Google Business profiles. Changes are detected weekly and you get alerts for price changes and new items.

What kinds of restaurants is this for?

Any independent restaurant doing $500K-$3M in annual revenue. Cuisine doesn't matter — trend data and invoice tracking work for Italian, Mexican, Asian, American, and everything between. Multi-location groups under 10 units work great too.

How accurate is the invoice parsing?

95%+ accuracy on major distributors (Sysco, US Foods, Restaurant Depot, Performance Food Group). For other suppliers, accuracy improves as you upload more invoices. You can always correct line items with one click.
