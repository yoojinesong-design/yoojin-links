# suna — "Orbit" Short-Form Music Video Kit (v2)

**Persona:** suna
**Song:** Orbit
**Sound:** Dreamy nocturnal R&B × Jersey club bounce — NewJeans "Supernatural" gravity, hypnotic pull
**Key Lyric Moments:**
- "you're the gravity I didn't ask for"
- "I don't orbit anyone, but here I am"
- "caught in your orbit"
- "at first it was nothing / then it was everything"
**Emotional Core:** Surrendering to someone's gravitational pull — the moment you realize you've already lost the fight against falling
**Best Musical Moment:** Hook drop where Jersey club kick pattern enters under airy vocals; bridge breakdown where drums strip away to pure synth haze

---

## 1. CONCEPT DIRECTION

차가운 네온 빛이 천천히 회전하는 밤의 도시. 모든 것이 원을 그리며 움직인다 — 헤드라이트, 물방울, 조명, 연기. 색감은 딥 인디고 + 일렉트릭 바이올렛, 간헐적으로 웜 앰버가 스파크처럼 터진다(= "그 사람"의 존재). 비주얼 속도는 곡의 BPM과 의도적으로 어긋나게 — 130 바운스 위에 드리프트하는 슬로우모션. 얼굴 없음. 무드가 주인공.
