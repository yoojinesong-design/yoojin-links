## 2. 8 SHORT-FORM CUTS

- **CTA:** 마지막 프레임 — "full track out now 🌙 (saved yet?)"
- **FORMAT:** Lyric visual

- **HOOK (0-3s):** *"POV: someone became your whole orbit and you didn't even notice"* + 도시 야경 흔들리는 핸드헬드

- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "at first it was nothing / then it was everything / then it was the only direction"
- **CTA:** "the song for that feeling → suna 'Orbit'"
- **FORMAT:** POV text story

### CUT 3 — Hook Loop (aesthetic-visual)
- **HOOK (0-3s):** 훅 부분 바로 재생 + "orbit" 글자가 실제로 원 그리며 도는 모션
